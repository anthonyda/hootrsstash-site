Privacy policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026. This policy applies to the HootRSStash app for Android.

In short: HootRSStash collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your feeds, articles and settings are stored on your phone only, and the developer never receives them.

What the app stores, on your phone

  • The list of feeds you add (address, name, site icon address);
  • the articles of those feeds (title, link, summary, image address, date) and whether you have read them;
  • your settings (refresh interval, theme, storage limits…);
  • a cache of downloaded images and feed responses, to save data and battery.

This data never leaves your phone through the app. Old articles are removed automatically according to your storage settings.

Network connections

The app connects directly to the websites of the feeds you add, and to the servers that host their images and site icons. It does not use any server of its own. Like any web request, these sites can see your IP address and the app's name (HootRSStash/1.0 (Android RSS reader)); their own privacy policies apply. When you open an article, it opens in your browser, on the publisher's site.

What the developer receives

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking library, and sends no usage or crash data.

Android backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include your feeds, articles and settings in your device backup (for example to your Google account), so they come back when you set up a new phone. This backup is handled by Android and your account provider, not by the developer. Images and caches are not included. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to download feeds and images, and to wait for Wi-Fi if you choose “Wi-Fi only”.

The app does not access your location, contacts, files, camera or microphone. The “Add feed” share option only reads the text or address you choose to share with it. Importing or exporting OPML only uses the file you pick.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete feeds in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app: everything it stored is removed from your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
